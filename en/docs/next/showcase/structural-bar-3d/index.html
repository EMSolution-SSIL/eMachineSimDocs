3D Bar Tension and Bending

This is a structural example without centrifugal force. Uniform surface traction is applied to a supplied 3D bar mesh by selecting boundary-face positions. It requires a licensed 0.2.0 or newer wheel; the 0.1.1 wheel does not include geometric face selection. Inputs and a runner are in Examples/structural/bar/.

Model and units​

The bar spans X/Y = 0..50 mm and Z = 0..100 mm, with 3,223 nodes and 16,202 TETRA4 volume elements. The verification material is linear isotropic elasticity with E = 210,000 N/mm2 and nu = 0.3. Forces are in N, traction and stress in N/mm2, and displacement in mm. These values do not represent a qualified motor component.

Fixed bottom, top-face tension​

input_bar_tension.json fixes all displacement components of the 121 nodes at Z=0. A uniform TZ = 1 N/mm2 acts on the top at Z=100. Z_MIN: 99.9 selects 200 faces with total area 2,500 mm2, giving 2,500 N total +Z force.

"STRUCTURAL_SURFACE_TRACTIONS": [
  {"TZ": 1.0, "Z_MIN": 99.9}
]
QuantityResult
Maximum displacement magnitude4.7099e-4 mm
Maximum von Mises stress1.2523 N/mm2

Local peak stress depends on the clamp and mesh. Do not equate the maximum with the nominal 2,500 N / 2,500 mm2 = 1 N/mm2 stress.

Fixed ends, side traction​

input_bar_bending.json fixes all components of the 242 nodes at both Z=0 and Z=100. The X=50 side receives TX = 1 N/mm2 in +X. The limits exclude the end faces and select 400 faces, carrying 5,000 N total.

"STRUCTURAL_SURFACE_TRACTIONS": [
  {"TX": 1.0, "X_MIN": 49.9, "Z_MIN": 0.1, "Z_MAX": 99.9}
]
QuantityResult
Maximum displacement magnitude5.4125e-4 mm
Maximum von Mises stress3.9323 N/mm2

The displayed shape is warped 10,000 times for visibility. The color bar and output.json report actual displacement magnitudes. Both ends remain fixed; displacement peaks around the middle.

Select faces and run​

TX/TY/TZ in STRUCTURAL_SURFACE_TRACTIONS are force per area. Positive PRESSURE in STRUCTURAL_SURFACE_PRESSURES acts opposite the outward normal. X/Y/Z/R_MIN/MAX test boundary-face centroids and may be combined with SMAT_ID. A selector matching no faces fails, so check the matched face count in check and the force sum in structural_load_balance.csv.

python Examples/structural/bar/run_bar.py --output-dir bar_results

bar_results/tension/ and bar_results/bending/ contain output.json, structural_displacement.vtk, and structural_load_balance.csv. These cases check load, clamp, and output behavior; they are not a mesh-convergence or mounting-stiffness validation. For internal pressure, see the full-circle 3D ring.
